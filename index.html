How this is worked out

  • Replay, not averages. For each year and each possible planting day, the crop is grown through the real weather: it must germinate (7-day mean temperature above the crop's minimum), find moisture (soil-water balance from rain and Hargreaves evapotranspiration), escape frost after emergence, avoid heat at flowering, and collect enough growing-degree days to mature in time.
  • Window. The recommended window is the run of planting dates with at least an 80% chance of success, weighting recent years most (15-year e-folding) so it reflects today's climate.
  • Risk score. 100 minus the average chance of success across the window.
  • Trends. Theil–Sen slopes with a Mann–Kendall test; a trend is called significant at p < 0.05.
  • Limits. Weather is a ~50 km grid average, so frost pockets, slopes and local soils are not seen; crop numbers are generic defaults, not your variety. Use this alongside local knowledge, not instead of it.
